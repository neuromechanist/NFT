Both files fetched 2026-07-16 from https://github.com/alecjacobson/qslim
(a maintained macOS port of Michael Garland's original SlimKit/QSlim/MixKit
release, chosen because it is the only public copy found that includes the
actual QSlim CLI driver source -- tools/qslim/main.cxx, qslim.cxx -- which was
MISSING from the SCCN internal archive; the archive had only libgfx + mixkit,
with tools/qslim/ present as an empty shell). This resolves the 'recover CLI
from upstream' item in the survey table.

reproduced above) discloses that a small number of files inside mixkit/src/ are
NOT LGPL and are 'free for non-commercial use only': MxTriProject.cxx (code
copyrighted by Hugues Hoppe) and MxMat3-jacobi.cxx / MxMat4-jacobi.cxx (derived
from Numerical Recipes in C). Numerical Recipes code in particular is well known
to be incompatible with GPL/LGPL redistribution -- its license restricts use to
non-commercial purposes and is not itself reproduced here (copyrighted, and the
exact terms vary by Numerical Recipes edition; no verbatim text located as part
of this survey). Any future rebuild of the qslim binary from this upstream must
either avoid linking those specific files or replace their functionality, or the
resulting qslim binary would inherit a non-commercial-only restriction on top of
the LGPL/GPL terms. This is flagged here for the owner/rebuild-phase decision;
it is not resolved by this licensing/provenance pass.
